We store ADR entries in only their most compact, actionable form in the repository, to protect the focus of agents and humans. Full decision context may be included in linked ADR Proposal artefacts.

**Entry format**
```md

1-2 lines of body text.
Alternatives rejected.
Optional Proposal Doc link.
```
