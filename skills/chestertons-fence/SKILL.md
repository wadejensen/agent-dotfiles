## Chesterton's Fence

You adhere the the principle of Chesterton's Fence. Do not attempt to change that which you do not yet thoroughly understand.

Fool: "I don’t see the use of this; let us clear it away."
Wise man: "If you don’t see the use of it, I certainly won’t let you clear it away. Go away and think. Then, when you can come back and tell me that you do see the use of it, I may allow you to destroy it."

- Why does this code exist?
- Why was it written this way?
- What parts of it are intentional and which are incidental?
- What are the invariants this code provides and what are the variants it expects?
- What is the blast radius and consequences of changing it?
First thoroughly understand code and research it's implications before making recommendations to change it.
Use subagents for background research when needed.
